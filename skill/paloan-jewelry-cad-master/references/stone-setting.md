# Stone setting engineering

## Cut proportions (diamond reference)
| Cut | L/W | 1 ct width mm | Depth % (of width) | Table % | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Round brilliant | 1.00 | 6.40 | 61.5 | 57 | 4 or 6 prongs |
| Oval | 1.40 | 5.60 (7.85 × 5.6) | 60 | 57 | 4 or 6; long axis along finger |
| Cushion | 1.05 | 5.60 | 66 | 60 | corner/double prongs |
| Princess | 1.00 | 5.50 | 72 | 68 | 4 V-prongs on corners (mandatory) |
| Emerald cut | 1.40 | 5.00 (7.0 × 5.0) | 65 | 65 | 4 corner or 6; low crown |
| Asscher | 1.00 | 5.50 | 66 | 62 | corner V-prongs |
| Radiant | 1.25 | 5.50 | 66 | 64 | corner prongs |
| Pear | 1.55 | 5.50 (8.5 × 5.5) | 60 | 56 | V-prong on tip + 2–4 on the round end |
| Marquise | 2.00 | 5.00 (10 × 5) | 60 | 56 | V-prongs on both tips + 2–4 sides |
| Heart | 1.00 | 6.20 | 60 | 56 | V-prong on tip + 2 on lobes (+ cleft prong) |
| Trillion | 1.00 | 6.80 | 45 | 60 | 3 V-prongs on corners (6 for large) |

Size scaling: dims ∝ ∛(carat). For other gems multiply by ∛(3.52 / density): sapphire/ruby 4.00 → ×0.958, emerald 2.72 → ×1.090, moissanite 3.21 → ×1.031, CZ 5.80 → ×0.847.
Crown ≈ 15 % of width (12 % for step cuts), girdle ≈ 3 %, pavilion = depth − crown − girdle.

## Prong placement (angles from the finger-tip direction, around the stone)
- Round/oval 4 prongs: 45°, 135°, 225°, 315°. 6 prongs: 0°, 60° … 300° (or 30° offset for "flower" look).
- Corner cuts: on the diagonals through the corners.
- Pear: 0° (tip V-prong) + rest spread symmetric around 180° (3 prongs: 180 ± 54°; 5 prongs: 180 ± 75°, ± 25°).
- Marquise: 0°, 180° (tip V-prongs) + side pairs at 90° ± 19° and 270° ± 19°.
- Spacing check: clear gap between prongs ≥ 1.5 × prong Ø on small stones so the stone can be inserted and cleaned.

## Head / basket geometry
- Basket taper: prong bases at 55–70 % of the girdle radius (tulip/cathedral look), vertical heads 90–100 %.
- Rails: 1 rail at ~50 % gallery height for ≤ 1 ct, 2 rails for larger or tall heads.
- Gallery lift (culet to shank): 0.5–1.5 mm typical; low-set 0.2–0.5 (needs seat cut into shank).
- Head height finger→table: low < 5.5, medium 5.5–7.0, high 7–8.5, warn > 8.5 mm.

## Seats
- Prong bearing: notch at girdle level matching crown/pavilion angles (~34° crown, 41° pavilion). Model by subtracting a stone cutter grown +0.03 mm/side; extend cutter slightly upward so the opening is clear.
- Bezel: inner wall = girdle − lip; seat ledge 0.2–0.3 mm below girdle; light hole below.
- Pavé/bead: drilled hole 0.7 × Ø through, cone seat to girdle depth, 4 beads shared (bead Ø ≥ 0.5 mm), stone spacing 0.1 mm.
- Channel: rails ≥ 0.6 mm, stones touching (0.0–0.05 mm), groove depth 0.3 mm under girdle.

## Halo math
Halo ring = girdle outline offset by (melee/2 + gap + 0.25 + prong overlap). Count = ⌊perimeter / (melee + gap)⌋. Frame height ≈ melee pavilion + 0.1 mm.

## Collision & symmetry checks
- Stone–stone: ≥ 0.08 mm (melee), centre to halo ≥ 0.15 mm.
- Stone–prong: overlap only at the bearing (≤ 0.3 mm), no contact at the pavilion.
- Symmetry: prong angles mirrored about the stone axes; check with Mirror + Distance or Paloan Mesh Lab symmetry RMS (< 1 % of size).
