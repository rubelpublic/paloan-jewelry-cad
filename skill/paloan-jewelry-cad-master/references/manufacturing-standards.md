# Manufacturing standards (lost-wax casting, 3D-printed resin/wax masters)

Values are production defaults used by Paloan's validator. Gold = 14–18K. Adjust: silver / 22–24K +0.2 mm on walls (softer, more porosity), platinum −10 % allowed on prongs (stronger, but casts thinner poorly — keep walls ≥ 0.5 mm), palladium like 18K white.

## Walls and sections
| Element | Fail | Warn | Recommended |
|---|---|---|---|
| Ring shank bottom thickness | < 1.0 | < 1.3 | 1.5–2.0 (wide bands 1.6+) |
| Ring band width | < 1.2 | < 1.5 | ≥ 1.8 |
| Bangle thickness | < 1.4 | < 1.8 | 2.0–2.8 |
| Generic cast wall | < 0.5 | < 0.6 | ≥ 0.7 (silver 0.8–1.0) |
| Pendant plate | < 0.8 | < 0.9 | 1.2–2.0 (or thicker + hollowed) |
| Hollow (box) wall | < 0.5 | < 0.6 | 0.7 |
| Hollow rails / edges | < 0.6 | < 0.8 | 1.0 |
| Filigree / coil wire | < 0.5 | < 0.6 | 0.7–0.9 |

## Setting hardware
- Prong Ø by smallest stone dimension: ≤ 3 mm → 0.6; ≤ 5 → 0.75; ≤ 7 → 0.9; ≤ 9 → 1.05; > 9 → 1.2 mm. Add +0.1 mm when using only 4 prongs on stones > 7 mm.
- Prong tip above girdle ≈ 60–75 % of crown height; tip bead Ø ≈ 1.05–1.1 × prong Ø.
- Bearing (seat) cut 0.1–0.3 mm into each prong (≤ 30 % of Ø). Cutter = stone + 0.03 mm all round (≈ +0.06 mm on L and W).
- Gallery rail Ø ≈ 0.7 × prong Ø, never touching the pavilion (≥ 0.1 mm clear).
- Culet: ≥ 0.2 mm above shank metal; never protrudes below the finger hole; leave a cleaning gap.
- Bezel: wall ≥ 0.5 mm (0.6–0.8 for daily wear), lip 0.15–0.5 mm over the girdle, height to 35 % of crown above girdle.
- Halo: melee gap 0.08–0.25 mm, shared beads ≥ 0.5 mm, frame width ≈ melee Ø + 0.6 mm.
- Pavé on a band: ≥ 0.25 mm metal each side of a stone row (band width ≥ stone + 0.5 mm), seat holes ≈ 0.7 × stone Ø through for light/cleaning.
- Open-back light hole under centre stones: girdle outline offset −0.4 to −0.6 mm.

## Ornament
- Spikes: base Ø ≥ 1.0 mm (wax breaks below); length/base ≤ 2.5:1 (fail > 3.6:1); sink base 0.25 mm into the body; tip radius ≥ 0.1 mm for wearer safety. Soft alloys: ≤ 2:1.
- Openwork bridges ≥ 0.8 mm (0.6 absolute min in gold, 1.0 in silver); holes ≥ 0.6 mm so investment can be removed; keep a solid rim ≥ 1.0–1.5 mm around pierced areas.
- Relief / popping carving: relief height 0.3–1.2 mm on a ≥ 0.8 mm base; undercuts must be printable/castable (no closed voids); minimum feature 0.3 mm.
- Milgrain beads Ø 0.3–0.45 mm (applied with a tool after casting — model optional).

## Mechanisms and findings
- Hinge: 3–5 knuckles, 0.05–0.15 mm clearance, pin Ø 0.8–1.0 mm, knuckle wall ≥ 0.4 mm around the pin.
- Box clasp tongue 0.4–0.5 mm spring sheet (not cast).
- Chain link: wire ≥ 0.6 mm gold; inner width > 2 × wire + 0.2 mm; pitch = inner length for cable chain.
- Jump ring inner ≥ 2 × chain wire + 0.3 mm.
- Ear post Ø 0.76–0.9 mm, 9–11 mm, work-hardened wire soldered after casting; friction back ~ 0.3 g (14K).
- Bail inner ≥ 3 mm (fits most chains and lobster clasps); bail wire ≥ 1.0 mm.

## Casting & printing
- Cast weight ≈ finished × 1.04 (sprue/polish loss 3–6 %).
- Resin print orientation: seats and prongs up; supports inside shank/back; 25–50 µm layers; export STL at 0.005 mm chord tolerance.
- Sprue at the heaviest section (shank bottom / pendant base), never on prongs or textured faces.
- Shrinkage: castable resin/wax → metal ≈ 1–2 %; size up ring-hole if critical (≈ +0.05 mm on Ø per % shrink).
- Avoid closed hollow voids (trapped investment) — every cavity needs an opening ≥ 1 mm.
